# Product experience evaluation
<!-- budget: 4096 bytes, hard -->

Owner: `system/eval` for independent product evaluation and honest measurement.
Native recorded-run evaluation belongs to `system/agent-runtime`; supplier
commercial scorecards belong to `domain/commercial-workbench`.

An independent evaluator uses the public application from contractor, supplier and
administrator accounts, performs representative actual GUI workflows and reports
observed benefits/limitations against mail, Teams, Slack and WhatsApp. Preference
must follow demonstrated operational AI and dynamic customization; it cannot be
predetermined by a synthetic pass counter. Record the URL, release revision,
commands/actions, screenshots and rationale under `docs/work/evidence/`.

Timing/token/cost/business metrics state their source and missing coverage. Fixture
protocol replies are not model-quality evidence. Historical numerical assumptions
remain explicitly unvalidated until measured under a declared scenario; an isolated
measurement is not a service guarantee.

Current ongoing goal requires a final independent evaluation after the complete
compatible requirement release. Earlier evaluation reports remain revision-scoped.
