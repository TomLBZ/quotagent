# Contractor guide
<!-- budget: 6144 bytes, hard -->

Start in your own contractor account. **Party team** lets a colleague explicitly
accept membership in your workspace; sharing a business role alone grants no
access. Select the intended workspace before starting work.

1. In **Requests**, create a project/section and an RFQ, or ask the assistant to
   prepare a draft. **Ingest documents** converts uploaded emails, spreadsheets and
   documents into editable lines. Check quantities, units, currency, deadlines,
   scope and invited parties before publishing. New publications need declared
   measurement rules, interface responsibilities, deliverables and exclusions.
   Confirm uncited extraction assumptions explicitly; edits need fresh confirmation.
2. Answer clarification tickets and review a broadcast before sending the answer
   to the invited parties. Reusable FAQs retain their source. Publish amendments
   explicitly; quotations bound to an older request revision stay visibly stale.
3. Compare current offers. **Commercial workbench** provides sourced TCO components,
   declared weights, FX observations and reference prices. Missing/stale rates or
   unpriced scope require a decision; warning flags do not silently change scores.
4. Prepare an award intent, obtain the supplier's confirmation, nominate an eligible
   independent colleague and then sign the reviewed commitment. The resulting PO
   retains the offered basis; it is not an agent-generated approval.
5. Review changes against original line rates. Record partial acceptance and
   deficiencies, then match an invoice to PO and accepted quantities. Mismatches
   remain in history when corrected.
6. Use **Object attachments** to keep an internal version or explicitly deliver an
   exact version to a business counterparty. **Message delivery** shows receipts,
   queued/held/conflicting transfers and recovery actions. A transmission receipt
   does not establish contractual acceptance.
7. Use **Evidence** to export a signed contiguous ledger range and relevant proofs.
   Give the reviewer the package and a public key trusted separately. A valid
   signature shows consistency with that key, not the truth of business assertions.

**Review actions**, **Party team**, notifications and the agent workroom support
handoffs, independent review, assigned work and explicit remembered preferences.
**Responses** tracks current coverage, reviewed reminders and response promises.
Its Business measures tab compares record-derived response cycles, completeness
and intervention counts across equal-duration windows, with definitions,
denominators and source links. Missing history stays unmeasured.
You can continue other work while a review waits. Supplier cost models and internal
supplier evaluations are not part of your received quote.

**Files & retention** previews eligibility, project/account constraints and holds.
Only the workspace owner can propose disposal of eligible local bytes, then approve
that exact list. Ledger facts, exported packages and counterparty copies remain.
