# Supplier guide
<!-- budget: 6144 bytes, hard -->

Your supplier account shows the requests delivered to your party and your own
quotations. It does not reveal competing bids, a contractor's private estimate or
another supplier's costs. Accept a colleague's workspace invitation explicitly
before working in that party's shared records.

1. Open a received request and inspect its current revision, line items, scope,
   deadlines and delivered attachments. Ask a clarification before relying on an
   ambiguous specification. Published answers and later amendments keep their
   source and history.
2. Ask the assistant to prepare a quote, or use the quotation editor. Uploaded files
   can enter **Ingest documents** for editable extraction. Check quantities, units,
   prices, tax mode, validity, lead time, payment, warranty and deviations.
3. In **Commercial workbench**, maintain your private material/labor/equipment and
   overhead/risk/tax/finance factors. Inspect margin, declared capacity and task
   dependencies. Applying proposed prices is reviewed and changes only a draft;
   it does not submit a quote. Private costs stay within your realm.
4. Review the exact quote before submission. A revised RFQ can make an earlier
   quote stale; reprice and resubmit against the new basis. Withdraw explicitly
   when you cannot stand behind an offer.
5. Respond to the contractor's award intent, then inspect and acknowledge the
   issued order. Changes, concessions and delivery dates follow their explicit
   human review paths. Receipt of a message alone is not your acceptance.
6. Deliver versioned object attachments to the relevant contractor when needed.
   Inspect **Message delivery** for failed, held or unconfirmed transfers before
   proposing another send. Retained versions remain downloadable while their bytes
   are available; a removal is recorded, not erased.

Use **Agent workroom** for a saved plan and specialist assistance. Pause, resume,
steer or stop the work; inspect the actual tool results and human checkpoints.
Explicit memory entries can capture preferences for future tasks. **AI usage**
shows token coverage and known/unknown cost rather than treating unknown price as
free.

You can create a personal theme, utility or reusable skill in **Plugin studio**,
inspect it, load/unload it and share it to the market. Published copies have their
own lineage. Only the separate administrator can promote a global default.
