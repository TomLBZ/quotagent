Never substitute quotes or terms from any other RFQ. This workflow never sends messages, publishes, awards, approves changes, or alters any record; it only reads and reports. Do not estimate, assume or interpolate any price, rate, lead time or term that is not present in the records. If a field is absent, call it NOT STATED and treat it as an unknown, never as included.
Step 1 - List submitted offers. Enumerate every quote submitted against the selected RFQ with supplier name, quote ID, status and submitted timestamp. If there are no submitted quotes, state that explicitly and stop the comparison; do not pull in quotes from other RFQs.
Step 2 - Verify line-item totals. For each quote recompute every line as quantity x unit price and compare with the stated line total. Then sum the lines and compare with the quote's stated total. Report every discrepancy as a flag with the exact figures (quote ID, line description, quantity, unit price, computed vs stated). Note any RFQ line item with no corresponding quoted line, and any quoted line that the RFQ did not request (scope coverage and short-supply check).
Step 3 - Distinguish included delivery and VAT from unknowns. For each quote classify delivery cost and VAT as one of: explicitly included, quoted as a separate line, explicitly excluded, or NOT STATED. Treat NOT STATED as an unknown and never as included; never assume a percentage. Note where delivery or VAT appears bundled into unit prices when the RFQ asked for them separately.
Step 4 - Compare lead time. Tabulate stated lead time in days per supplier against the RFQ's required delivery date. Flag every lead time that would miss the required date, and flag every quote with no lead time stated.
Step 5 - Flag exclusions and warranty gaps. Record each supplier's stated exclusions as written (offloading, installation, commissioning, waste disposal, access restrictions, phased deliveries) and mark unstated items as unknown. Record stated warranty length per supplier; if the RFQ set no minimum warranty, say that no minimum is set; flag missing or vague warranty responses and any substitutions offered.
Step 6 - Draft one clarification question per supplier. Produce exactly one targeted question per supplier, aimed at that supplier's most decision-critical gap (delivery or VAT treatment, lead time, warranty, unstated exclusions, or spec ambiguity). Present it as draft text only, clearly labelled 'draft - not sent'. Do not call any message-sending or commitment action.
Step 7 - Identify missing data. Close with a consolidated list split into missing on the RFQ side (currency, submission deadline, spec gaps) and missing from supplier responses, clearly separating facts taken from records from gaps.
Output format: a compact report opening with a scope line (RFQ ID and title, quote count, suppliers, review timestamp) and the statement that nothing was changed; then a per-supplier comparison table with columns Supplier | Quote ID | Status | Submitted | Quoted total | Line-item arithmetic check | Delivery treatment | VAT treatment | Lead time (days) | Warranty | Exclusions | Substitutions; then numbered flags; then the draft clarification questions; then the missing-data list. Cite RFQ IDs, quote IDs and supplier names throughout. Use deterministic arithmetic only from the selected RFQ's records, and never invent offers, prices or terms.
Workflow steps:
1. Confirm the currently selected RFQ and pull only its linked records (items, quotes, suppliers, messages, changes); state that the run is read-only.
2. Enumerate every submitted quote with supplier name, quote ID, status and submitted timestamp; if none exist, say so and stop the comparison.
3. Recompute each quote line as quantity x unit price against the stated line total, then sum the lines against the stated quote total, flagging every discrepancy with exact figures.
4. Check scope coverage: RFQ lines with no quoted line, and quoted lines that were not requested.
5. Classify delivery cost and VAT per quote as explicitly included, separate line, explicitly excluded, or NOT STATED; flag bundling into unit prices.
6. Tabulate stated lead time in days per supplier against the RFQ required delivery date; flag misses and missing lead times.
7. Record stated exclusions and warranty per supplier, mark unstated items unknown, and flag vague or missing warranty plus any substitutions.
8. Draft exactly one clarification question per supplier targeting that supplier's most decision-critical gap, labelled draft - not sent.
9. Compile missing data split into RFQ-side (currency, submission deadline, spec gaps) and supplier-side gaps.
10. Emit the comparison table, flags, draft questions and missing-data list, and confirm that no record was created, sent, published, awarded or changed.
